Opens the logo in the editor in its thread colors, so you can add text, erase parts or move things around.

  • Sew a test on scrap fabric with stabilizer before the real garment.
  • White thread looks faint on the machine's screen. That's normal.
  • Your machine will stop at each thread change and show the color name listed above.
